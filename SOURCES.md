# Sources and prior work

Searches were updated on 19 October 2026. They were targeted rather than exhaustive.

- Zeng, Liu and Ratnavelu proposed the coefficient 13/48 and the matrix `J2 direct-sum E3`, proving structured cases and local maximality. This release addresses their stated global real rank-three problem: https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202608.1240.v1
- Li, Zhao and Luo claim the coefficient-one Chollet inequality for arbitrary complex Hermitian positive-semidefinite matrices through order six. The domain is broader but the coefficient is weaker, so it does not imply the sharp result here: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202608.2196
- Gatermann and Parrilo established symmetry reduction for sum-of-squares calculations: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpaa.2003.12.011
- Peyrl and Parrilo established rational reconstruction techniques for numerical sum-of-squares certificates: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2008.09.025
- Rodtes proved the order-four Chollet conjecture: https://doi.org/10.1080/03081087.2023.2279150
- Ma gives recent rank-four quantitative results in different orders and subclasses: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37461

No earlier complete proof of the identical sharp real order-five rank-at-most-three theorem was found in the inspected corpus. This is a bounded search result, not universal priority certification.
